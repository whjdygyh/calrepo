BOUNDARY

Scope and coexistence

This concise calendar includes major seasons, solemnities, feasts, and selected universal saints. It excludes Ordinary Time Sundays and routine observances. It may be subscribed alongside the complete General Roman Calendar for pastoral or liturgical use; shared observances can then appear in both calendars.
